My programming journey started at university with C and C++. To be honest, it felt quite dry at first. Dealing with pointers, memory management, and terminal outputs wasn't exactly the flashy hacker experience I had imagined. 

However, looking back, it was the perfect crucible. It built a strong foundation of logical thinking and algorithmic problem-solving that would become invaluable later on.

<Callout type="default">
  "The hardest part is understanding pointers. Once you get that, everything else is just syntax."
</Callout>
