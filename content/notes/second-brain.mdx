# What is a Second Brain?

A Second Brain is a methodology for saving and systematically reminding us of the ideas, inspirations, insights, and connections we've gained through our experience.

This system is built using the principles of a [Digital Garden](/brain/digital-garden), where ideas can grow over time rather than being published once and forgotten.
