# The Vision

Personal Universe is not just another portfolio or blog template. It is designed to be a living, breathing extension of a creator's mind on the internet. Instead of organizing content chronologically like a traditional blog, it organizes knowledge spatially and relationally.

## Key Features

- **Content Engine**: High-performance Markdown/MDX parsing and rendering with frontmatter validation.
- **Second Brain**: A digital garden where notes are interlinked, forming a network of ideas.
- **3D Knowledge Graph**: A visual representation of the notes network using Three.js and Force-Directed Graphs.
- **Interactive Lab**: Dedicated spaces for mini-experiments, UI prototypes, and physics simulations.
