After getting comfortable with the basics, I decided it was time to build something real. That's when I discovered **C#** and **ASP.NET**. 

Moving from C++ to a modern, powerful backend framework was a breath of fresh air. I started building RESTful APIs, connecting to databases, and understanding how the web truly works behind the scenes. The structured nature of C# and the robust ecosystem of .NET made me fall in love with backend development.

This phase marked my transition from a student learning logic to a developer building actual software systems.
