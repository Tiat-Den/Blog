# Digital Gardens

Digital gardens explore the idea of a public workspace. Unlike a blog, which presents finished, polished thoughts chronologically, a digital garden is topographical. You wander through it.

Thoughts are planted as seeds (short, unpolished notes) and cultivated over time into trees (comprehensive articles).

This ties closely into the concept of building a [Second Brain](/brain/second-brain).
