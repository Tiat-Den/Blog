While backend development is logically satisfying, I realized I wanted to build applications that are not just functional, but also visually stunning. I wanted to craft experiences that users can see and interact with directly.

This desire led me to the frontend world. I decided to learn **Next.js** and modern web development stack (React, Tailwind CSS). The transition from strict backend logic to the dynamic and creative world of UI/UX design was challenging but incredibly rewarding.

This very blog, the **Personal Universe**, is the culmination of that effort. It serves as my digital playground where I combine robust data structures with beautiful, interactive interfaces.
